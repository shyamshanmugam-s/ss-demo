## 3. Case Study Content Clarity Audit

Across all 6 project dossiers:

1. **Strategic Intent**: Every project clearly articulates the business challenge (e.g. PDF technical catalogs in manufacturing, uninspired property listings in real estate).
2. **Technical Grounding**: Verified technology stacks (React, TypeScript, GSAP, Lenis, Vite) are declared with exact technical specifications.
3. **Interactive Demonstrations**: Product wizards, RFQs, and reservation flows are explicitly marked as frontend demonstrations with disclaimers.
4. **Zero Misleading Claims**: No unverified client testimonials, fake revenue metrics, or false deployment claims exist in the portfolio.
